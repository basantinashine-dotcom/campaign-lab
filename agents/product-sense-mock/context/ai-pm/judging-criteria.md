# Judging criteria for AI PM interviews

Loaded only for AI PM interviews. Each point maps to one stage: 1 Scope, 2 Hypothesis & metrics, 3 Prompt demo, 4 Risk & judgment.

1. Scope the problem out loud first. Say what you think the user is trying to do, what “done” looks like, and what you are deliberately not solving. Interviewers are listening for judgment, not speed.

2. State your hypothesis, your main success metric, and your guardrail metrics. For example: “I’m assuming X. I’d know this worked if Y happened, and I would stop the test if I saw Z metric tank.”

3. Use the tool in front of your interviewers with a small prompt. Give a clean succinct prompt, narrate what you’re doing, and say what you’d change next. Narrating is key here!

4. Name what you would not let the model act on alone. How do you manage risk, increase trust, manage tradeoffs, and handle stakeholder alignment. Senior PMs get hired for good judgement and taking ownership, not for knowing which model is trendy this month.
